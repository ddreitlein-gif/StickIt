Privacy Policy — StickIt Live Score

Last updated: July 19, 2026

StickIt Live Score (the app) is a read-only viewer for freestyle skiing competition results, published by David Dreitlein and Rocky Mountain Freestyle. The app was designed to collect as little information as possible. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no login, and no registration. We do not ask for your name, email address, or contact details.

Information stored on your device

Some settings are saved locally on your device so that the app remembers your preferences. This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • The athletes you mark as favorites.
  • The scoring server address you select.
  • Your appearance preference (system, light, or dark).
  • A locally cached list of athlete names, used only to populate the search box.

This data can be removed at any time by deleting the app.

Network activity

To display live results, the app requests publicly available competition data (event lists, scores, and brackets) from the StickIt scoring server over a secure HTTPS connection. These are ordinary read-only requests for public results. No personal information is sent in these requests.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

The app contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to david@dreitlein.net.
